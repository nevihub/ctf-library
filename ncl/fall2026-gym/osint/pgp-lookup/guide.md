# PGP Lookup — walkthrough summary

_Official Trove guide: https://trove.cyberskyline.com/8eb49cf698d149fe969acd161020245d (login required)_

## Technique

Query keyserver.ubuntu.com, keys.openpgp.org, and pgp.mit.edu and compare results. The fingerprint follows 'rsa4096/', the email sits right of 'uid', and expiry is the 'key expir' column (not creation time).
