# WiFi PCAP 3

- Category: Network Traffic Analysis
- Difficulty: Hard
- Points: 100

## Description

Crack the WPA key and analyze the third Wi-Fi capture.

- What is the ESSID? (10 pts)

- What is the router IP? (10 pts)
- What is the manufacturer? (10 pts)
- What is the model? (10 pts)
- What is the firmware? (10 pts)
- What is the release? (10 pts)
- What is the admin-login IP? (10 pts)
- What are the two deauth victim MACs? (10 pts)
