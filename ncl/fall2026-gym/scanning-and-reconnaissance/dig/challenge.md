# Dig

- Category: Scanning & Reconnaissance
- Difficulty: Easy
- Points: 75

## Description

Use the dig tool to look up DNS records for the fortaigan.net domain through the provided resolver.

## Questions

- What is the IPv4 address of fortaigan.net? (10 pts)
- How many mail servers are there? (10 pts)
- What is the first-priority MX record? (10 pts)
- What is the IPv4 address of the name server? (10 pts)
- Who is the responsible person? (10 pts)
- What is the TXT flag? (10 pts)
- What is the SIP TCP priority? (10 pts)
- What is the IPv4 address of the SIP host? (5 pts)
