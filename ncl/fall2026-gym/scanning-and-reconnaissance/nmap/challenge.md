# NMAP

- Category: Scanning & Reconnaissance
- Difficulty: Easy
- Points: 85

## Description

Scan the provided live target to enumerate open ports and identify the software running on them.

## Questions

- What are the three lowest open TCP ports? (30 pts)
- What is the lowest open UDP port? (30 pts)
- What software (name and version) runs on TCP port 16080? (25 pts)
