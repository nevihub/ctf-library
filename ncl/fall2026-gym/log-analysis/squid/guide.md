## Technique

Convert the epoch timestamps to get the log year; aggregate the response-time, client-IP and method columns for fastest/longest times, distinct IPs and GET/POST counts; identify the antivirus company from the update URLs fetched by 192.168.0.224 and report its AV update URL.
