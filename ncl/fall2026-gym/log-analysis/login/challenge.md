# Login

- Category: Log Analysis
- Difficulty: Easy
- Points: 100

## Description

Analyze the login.log of login attempts.

## Questions

- How many total login attempts were there? (20 pts)
- How many unique usernames were tried? (15 pts)
- What is the top username? (15 pts)
- How many attempts used the top username? (15 pts)
- What was the busiest date? (15 pts)
- Which username came from the most unique IPs? (20 pts)
